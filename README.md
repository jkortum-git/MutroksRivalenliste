# Mutroks Rivalenliste

Keep a personal list of rival players in WoW Forever. Click the minimap crosshair to add a character by first name and surname, save a note, or import your current player target. Edit and remove entries in the same window.

When a listed player appears on a visible nameplate, under your mouse, or as your target in the open world, the addon alerts you with a sound, a screen message and your note. Manually entered names stay unconfirmed until you encounter the player. NPCs do not trigger alerts. Your list is saved locally across your characters. The interface is in German.
